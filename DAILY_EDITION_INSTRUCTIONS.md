HOW THE SITE IS LAID OUT (so you write the right content)
- Masthead: football logo centered, the A.N.A.L. name under it, "Aniston News Always Live" under that. Black, hot pink and orange.
- Tabs: Home, Recap, Power Bottoms, M.I.L.F., F.U.P.A., Climax Bracket, Aniston Hour, Payouts, Hall of Fame, R.I.P., Contacts for Your Mom. A "Let's talk Butt Stuff" button appears under the menu only when "butt" is non-empty.
- Front page, top to bottom:
  1. Two boxes: Weekly Payout (this week's category, last week's winner, from "payouts") and Climax Bracket ("My favorites to win the Aniston": the two projected finalists, then the projected champion, from "climax").
  2. Top Stories: the lead (stories[0]) with headline, dek and "Read the full story", and a side list of the other stories.
  3. The Picks: three cards (Matchup of the Week, Shit Show of the Week, Blowie of the Week) from "picks", each linking to its full write-up.
  4. Live Cams: three cards linking to F.U.P.A., M.I.L.F. and the Climax Bracket (fixed in index.html; no data needed).
  5. The Hot Seat: Start Trying and Go Fuck Yourself (from "hotseat").
  6. Quick Matchups: one card per "lines" entry with spread, total, win % and a short synopsis taken from the note (the sentence starting "Final:" or "Live:" plus the next sentence; if neither exists, the first two sentences), with "Full matchup" linking to that game on M.I.L.F. Then Under Wire (first sentence of the first 6 "wire" items).
  7. From the Desk: the four columns as cards with columnist photo, a "Rumors inside" tag, and "The whisper" (a sentence pulled from the column's rumor paragraph).
  8. Standings.
- Power Bottoms: power rankings ("rankings") where each team expands into its team file ("teams": record, PF, all-play, this week's matchup, since, titles, body, injuries, past lives).
- M.I.L.F. (Matchups, Injuries, Lines & Forecasts): the three picks cards, last week's pick review, then every line with its full note.
- F.U.P.A. (Fuck You Projections & Analysis): every story and every column, plus the staff list.
- Climax Bracket: the projected playoff run left to right (Wild Card, Semifinals, Championship, The Aniston), each winner with its win %, then a projected-seeds table and the first three teams out.
- Recap: the latest week's biggest blowout, the feature article, and week-by-week Blowout / Bad Beat / Shit the Bed.
- Payouts, Hall of Fame (with awards and history), R.I.P. (ring banners plus "Where Is He Now?"), Aniston Hour (videos), Contacts for Your Mom (commish page).
- Every story and column has its own link: #story-<id> and #col-<id>, so keep ids short and stable.

CONTENT (data.json keys)
- updated: e.g. "Sunday, Oct 25, 2026 · 6:45 AM ET".
- week: the current/upcoming NFL week.
- ticker: last completed week's final scores as [winner, winner score, loser, loser score] (fallback only; the top bar shows lines when they have prob).
- wire: 6-8 dated, sourced news items that matter to league rosters, each {t, src, url}. Put the key fact in the first sentence (the front page shows only that). Items with src "League chat" come from the chat; keep any still current (under ~3 days old) and replace stale ones.
- stories: 4 stories, each {id, kicker, title, dek, body (3-4 paragraphs)}. stories[0] is the front-page lead. Refresh the lead daily; on Tuesdays (after Monday night) lead with the weekly recap.
- WAIVERS: every day, one of the stories (or a clearly separate section of one) reports waiver activity apart from roster and team notes: pull /transactions for the current and previous week and list each waiver claim (manager, player added, player dropped, FAAB bid) and each free-agent add as their own items, apart from trades and injury notes, plus FAAB spent by team.
- recap: weeks array for all completed weeks, each {w, blowout (largest margin of victory), beat, bed}; feature = {title, body} article on the most recent completed week.
- standings: [team, manager, record, PF, PA, all-play, streak], sorted by wins then PF.
- rankings (shown on Power Bottoms): {rk, prev (last edition's rk), team, own, rec, ap, take}.
- teams (the team files on Power Bottoms): keep each team's key; update rec, pf, ap, w7 (this week's opponent and line; keep the field name "w7" in every week), inj (current injuries for that team's players), body (2 paragraphs).
- hotseat: {trying: 3 × [team, text], gfy: 3 × [team, text]}.
- lines: one entry per current-week matchup {fav, dog, spread ("PK" or "-N.5"), total, note, prob}. Spread = season PPG difference, nudged for injuries/byes (explain any nudge in the note). Total = sum of the two PPGs. prob = favorite's win chance as an integer percent, computed in code as round(100 * Phi(|spread| / 31)) (PK = 50). Once games start, add a sentence beginning "Live:" with the current score, or "Final:" with the final score; the front-page card shows that sentence and the next one.
- picks: {"week": N, "review": {"w": N-1, "items": [{"label", "hit": true/false, "result"}], "note"}, "items": [3]}. One item each of type "motw" (label "Matchup of the Week": the biggest game, usually the highest total or highest stakes), "shit" (label "Shit Show of the Week": the ugliest game, usually the lowest total or the worst teams), "blowie" (label "Blowie of the Week": the biggest spread). Each item: id ("pick-motw", "pick-shit", "pick-blowie"), type, label, fav, dog, spread, total, prob (copied from the matching lines entry), pick ("<fav> by <spread without the minus>"), title, dek, body (3 paragraphs with real numbers: the case on paper, the line and any nudge, and what has happened so far once games start). Pick the three games at the start of each week and keep them all week, updating the bodies as games play. On Tuesdays, grade last week's three picks in review.items (Hit/Miss with final scores); if there's nothing to grade, put a short note in review.note.
- climax: the projected playoff run at the end of the regular season, NOT "if the playoffs started today". In python: simulate the remaining regular-season weeks (through playoff_week_start - 1) 20,000 times using the real schedule, each team's 2026 season PPG and a normal score with sd 24; count a current-week game that is still in progress for the team leading. Seeds = teams ranked by projected (average) wins, then projected PF. Bracket, 6 teams: Wild Card 4 vs 5 and 3 vs 6; Semifinals 1 vs the 4/5 winner and 2 vs the 3/6 winner; then the final. Each game's winner = the higher-PPG team, p = round(100 * Phi((PPG_winner - PPG_loser) / (24 * sqrt(2)))). Store: {"note": one sentence on the method, "seeds": [{"seed", "team", "now" (current record), "proj" (projected wins, 1 decimal), "po" (playoff odds %)}] × 6, "out": [{"team", "now", "proj", "po"}] × the next 3, "wc": [{"a", "b", "w", "p"} for 4v5, then for 3v6], "semis": [{1 vs W45}, {2 vs W36}], "final": {"a", "b", "w", "p"}, "champ": the final's winner, "favs": [{"team", "odds"}] for the two finalists, champion first}.
