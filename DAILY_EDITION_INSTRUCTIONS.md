- Rumor Mill page = columns + "Teams are considering trading these players" (the old trades suggestions, data.json "trades") + fake rumors + Locker Room Report (exits) + Hot Seat.
- Recap page opens with "Blowie of the Week" (the latest week's blowout line from recap.weeks, with a blurred graphic), then the feature article. Keep each recap week's "blowout" as the largest margin of victory.
- Trade Court page = trade history from data.json "tradehist" (see below).
- Hall of Fame (gold sparkle), R.I.P. (cemetery, plus "Where Is He Now?" from ring.banners[].wihn), Contacts for Your Mom (the commish page; keep commish.title = "Contacts for Your Mom").
